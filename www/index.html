Parameters

Widths, framings and twist counts are polynomials in the parameters, such as n+1, 2m-1 or n(n+1)/2. The handle data on the right holds for all values at once; tables and PD codes use the ranges.

Moves

    Cancelling a dotted circle with a 2-handle that passes through it once first slides every other strand through the circle over that handle, then erases both. It acts on whole bundles, so the result holds for every member of the family.
